# Code of conduct

This is a small project. The rules are short.

## Expected

- Be civil. Disagree with the argument, not the person making it.
- Assume the other person is acting in good faith until they prove otherwise.
- Keep discussion on the technical question at hand.
- Accept that "no" is a legitimate answer to a feature request, and that
  maintainer time is finite and unpaid.

## Not tolerated

- Harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning comments — including about
  someone's identity, background, or experience level.
- Deliberately publishing someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption: reopening settled arguments, brigading, or badgering
  maintainers for a response.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and commit messages in this
repository.

## Enforcement

Report a problem by opening a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/burnett-madoc-corp/claude-usage-menubar/security/advisories/new)
— it is the only private channel this repository has, and it works for conduct
reports as well as vulnerabilities. Reports are handled by the maintainer,
[@alecburnett](https://github.com/alecburnett), and kept confidential.

Responses range from a warning to a block from the repository, at the
maintainer's discretion and in proportion to the behaviour.
